Scoring rules & evidence

Indices v1 use campaign medians and the weakest component across both directions, all load phases and any recorded reverse leg. They are transparent reference indices, not probabilities or statistically significant rankings. Ties are retained at one decimal place. Only complete, compatible normalized journeys can be ranked together.

  • Useful progress: 100 × the lowest of achieved/requested results per second, ready-slot fraction, and unblocked-input fraction. Each fraction is capped at 1. Missing or failed media evidence prevents a score.
  • Smooth movement: 100 × the lower of 16.67ms / slow-frame interval and the fraction of frames no longer than 50ms. The reference is 60Hz; this is not measured input latency.
  • Stable pictures: 100 × the lower of retained-image readiness and 1 / (1 + geometry change beyond a 1px allowance). Geometry includes cell and image-element movement/resize after accounting for input. Changed sources prevent ranking pending inspection; node replacement alone is not a defect.

Stability is unscored when geometry sampling gaps exceed 250ms or sources change. Unfinished waits, observer cost and unmeasured paint remain visible in the evidence. Missing values never become zero. Old workloads, different environments and different coordinate regimes are not pooled into rankings.

How to read this evidence

One campaign per environment is a baseline, not a trend. Repetitions within a campaign are not additional historical points. With four repetitions, campaign p95 is the slowest repetition. Placement and visible-media clocks share the same start; do not add them.

D1–D7 are unthrottled. Revision-4 D8 constrains all three load steps and recovery (80ms / 4Mibit/s); earlier profiles remain separate. D11 also uses constrained delivery. Browser cache policy does not control server/proxy caches. Missing/censored metrics are unavailable, never zero. Layout-shift sums are not official CLS; attributed source nodes do not establish causality. Virtualized element reuse is not logical reordering.

Compatibility is enforced automatically: environment, workload, cache, regime, profile and measurement contract remain separate. Source revisions remain provenance. Table's narrow image column makes whole-viewport blank percentages particularly easy to underestimate. Charts share a numeric scale within each workload; different workloads may use different scales. Histories never connect incompatible series.
